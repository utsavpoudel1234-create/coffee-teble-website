Supporting local people with practical help, connection, and welcoming community programs.

The Coffee Table is a community services organisation that brings people together through support programs, social connection, learning opportunities, and referrals to local assistance. This website helps visitors find important information quickly and understand how they can access support.

Welcome to a caring local space

Our website brings together the most important community information in one place so people can find services, check centre details, read about community experiences, and reach out for help without confusion.

About Us

Helping people feel supported, informed, and connected

The Coffee Table exists to create a welcoming environment where individuals and families can access help, build confidence, and connect with the wider community. The website reflects this by using a clear layout, readable content, and easy pathways to programs and contact information.

Centres

Our five community centres

Each centre provides a welcoming space for different kinds of support, activities, and community connection.

Centre 01

Main Support Hub

General enquiries, first contact support, food relief information, and referrals to other local services.

Centre 02

Community Kitchen Centre

Shared meal services, food connection activities, and a warm space for people to meet and feel welcome.

Centre 03

Skills and Sewing Studio

Creative and practical workshops that support learning, confidence building, and personal development.

Centre 04

Neighbourhood Meeting Space

Community gatherings, family sessions, support groups, and local information events.

Centre 05

Outreach and Volunteer Desk

Volunteer coordination, partnership enquiries, outreach planning, and community engagement activities.
